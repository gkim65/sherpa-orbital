No code was written this session. The session was deliberately kept to understanding and auditing.

**Decision: `scripts/` folder for plots.** User wants plotting functions to be reusable and callable
by others. A `scripts/` folder with standalone Python scripts (not test files) is the right place.
Each script should be runnable directly (`python scripts/plot_orbit.py`) and produce a saved PNG.

**Target figure: Exhibit B-21 (MacKenzie §B.2.3).** This is the three-panel figure showing:
- Panel (a): x-z plane trajectory of the period-3 L1 halo (the "butterfly" shape)
- Panel (b): x-y groundtrack with periapsis altitude bands 20–70 km highlighted
- Panel (c): periapsis altitude vs elapsed days over 24 revolutions (~12 days), showing
  CR3BP (black) vs high-fidelity (blue) — we will do CR3BP vs CR3BP+J2

The axes in the paper use units of R_Enceladus (252.1 km). Our plots should support both
km and R_Enc units.

**J2 magnitude note:** At the test IC (periapsis ~1570 km altitude, ~1822 km from Enceladus
centre), J2/monopole ≈ 10⁻⁴. At the real mission orbit periapsis (~40 km altitude,
~292 km from Enceladus centre), J2/monopole ≈ (252/292)² × 5.4×10⁻³ ≈ 4×10⁻³.
This is where J2 is physically meaningful and explains the 12.84 km drift over 30 days.
